# History Optimizer

Use this skill when the task is to turn one-day analysis into a continuous improvement loop.

## Workflow

1. Archive the daily premarket plan.
2. Archive the daily postmarket review.
3. Update the history index.
4. Generate a compact history summary.
5. Extract optimization suggestions from repeated patterns.

## Focus

- whether attack bias is too frequent
- whether high position usage exceeds strong-market frequency
- whether weak-market days are expanding
- whether mainline stability is improving over time
