# Position Risk Manager

Use this skill when the task is to decide how much to trade, not just what to trade.

## Workflow

1. Read [A股短线情绪交易每日执行模板](/g:/A股游资/游资知识库/A股短线情绪交易每日执行模板-31c5a5db30.md).
2. Position comes after environment and mainline confirmation.
3. Favor:
   - `7成仓` only in clear attack conditions with strong leader and mainline
   - `半仓` in workable but not perfect conditions
   - `轻仓` or `空仓` in retreat, panic, or unclear-mainline conditions
4. Always attach retreat and add-position signals to the result.
